Simple Pasta Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil water and cook pasta until soft.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Add garlic and salute for one minute.
  4. Add tomato sauce and salt.
  5. Mix cooked pasta with the sauce.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy.
